About

Hello. I'm a computational materials scientist specializing in the mechanical and physical behavior of metallic materials. I received my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from Santa Clara University under the supervision of Professor Panthea Sepehrband. I have experience in atomistic simulations (molecular dynamics), machine learning (graph neural networks), mechanical testing, and materials characterization. My work has focused on contact formation, interfaces, diffusion, and deformation in metals, with applications in joining processes, additive manufacturing, and lattice metamaterials.
